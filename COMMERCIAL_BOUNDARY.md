## Commercial pilots

Commercial pilots require written scope before any buyer-specific work begins.

A commercial scope must define:

- buyer use case
- effect-bearing movement
- admissibility conditions
- authority boundary
- refusal, escalation, and halt paths
- customer-data boundary
- receipt and replay expectations
- production gates
- IP and licensing terms

Production review must address authentication, RBAC, tenant isolation, persistent audit and receipt storage, signing keys or KMS, deployment hardening, monitoring, alerting, incident response, and external security review.
